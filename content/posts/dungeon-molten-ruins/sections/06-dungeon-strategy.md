To be honest, I only have a solid strategy for the archer class in this dungeon.

As an archer, the goal is to strike a balance between crowd control and damage. You’ll want to focus on maximizing cooldown reduction to achieve this balance. The idea is simply to avoid getting hit at all—it may sound crazy, but it works!

For free-to-play players, there aren’t any specific tips aside from using the classic strategy of combining Thorns, Snail, Disarm, and Blitz. You’ll need to time these abilities correctly. Also, equip the Red Slime.

For pay-to-win players, I suggest using Duck Back, the car (or whale) as your mount, and either Flaming Carnage or the Arc artifact for maximum damage. With these elements and max cooldown reduction, the boss should hardly touch you, allowing you to push the dungeon further than you normally could.
