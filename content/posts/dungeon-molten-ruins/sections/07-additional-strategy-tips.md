You can run this dungeon manually and at normal speed (without acceleration). Why? Because the boss’s attack animation is very slow, allowing you to manually activate your Blitz skill at the last second.

What I used to do was set the auto mode at the start, with Disarm triggering after 7-8 seconds to ensure it lands after the first wave. I would cast Thorns at 0 seconds, and typically, the boss would be defeated before they had a chance to hit me.

Pay also attention to the fact that bosses have different size and thus they'll need a different time to reach the position to be able to hit you, that's why the third and fifth wave are the hardest ones.
