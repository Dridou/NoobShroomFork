In this dungeon, you have 1 minute and 20 seconds to clear 5 waves, each consisting of a drake boss. It’s important to keep track of time because, at a certain point (for me, at level 138 with 75 million power), I sometimes fail to finish due to running out of time rather than dying.

To succeed, you need to understand the dragon’s characteristics. The dragon has relatively low health but hits extremely hard, so don’t expect to tank it. However, its movement speed is medium, which allows you to control it effectively.

The key is not lost if you fail.
