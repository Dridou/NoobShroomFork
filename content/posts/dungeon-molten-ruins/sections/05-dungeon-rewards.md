At the maximum level (150), the dungeon rewards you with around 45 Pal and Skill tickets per run, increasing by 1 ticket every 4-5 levels. It’s worth pushing beyond these milestones to optimize your gains.

Although tickets drop from various sources, in this dungeon, let’s say you can collect about 40 Pal and Skill tickets x10 per day, which totals 400. These are crucial for ranking well in Pal and Skill rush events, and they also significantly boost your power by filling up your album and leveling up your Pals/Skills.
