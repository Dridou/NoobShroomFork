Two special ressources are winnable in this mode: crystals and points.

Crystals are limited and can be obtained by completing various tasks during the Crossdown. These crystals allow you to buy rare rewards.I recommend prioritizing the spells needed for your class. After that, I usually opt for hammers in priority.

Points can be earned infinitely on Players, and are limited in PvE. Initially, when you have few points it is recommended to buy in this order: pickaxes > lamps > souls then coupons (for rushes). When you have a lot, buy everything.
