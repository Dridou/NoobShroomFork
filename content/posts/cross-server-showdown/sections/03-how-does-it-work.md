You can invade one of the 3 servers for free each day between 10 AM and 10 PM (by launching the attack), then it costs 2000 red gems. You can go to your server for free as many times as you want and for unlimited time.

You then have 10 minutes base + 3 minutes if you defeat at least 3 people in PvP to score as many points as possible. You can teleport to any of the 5 possible territories, knowing that the higher the territory, the more points the monsters give, but it does not affect the points earned in PvP.

When you kill 3 or more people from the server, all connected players, even if they are not in this mode, will receive a notification with your name and the territory you are in, so be prepared to be attacked.
