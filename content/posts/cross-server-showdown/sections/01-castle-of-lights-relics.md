Regarding these relics, they are present in the Castle of Light territory.
These relics are the key to the Cross-Server Showdown, both for the server's victory and your personal points.Removing 100% of a relic's HP gives 50k points.

The battle against a relic lasts 1 minute, and the cooldown to attack it again is very short, and you won't be detected when hitting the relics unlike when attacking players.

These points are not limited to 4 relics, meaning if a relic is recaptured by the server you are invading during your invasion time, you can score more than 200k points in total.

Make sure you are in PvE class with a full offensive build to score as many points as possible.
