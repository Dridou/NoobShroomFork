The ideal back accessory for the Beastmaster is still under consideration, but accessories that boost attack, HP, shielding, or energy regeneration are generally beneficial. Currently, I use Cosmic Rescue for its effective shielding and attack boost that trigger as my HP decreases. This synergizes well with the Beastmaster’s passive ability, which increases pal damage by 4% for every 10% of missing HP, allowing you to maintain lower health levels and maximize damage output

For free-to-play players, Lustrous Plumage is an excellent choice. It enhances both your defense and damage, providing a valuable balance of survivability and increased output.
