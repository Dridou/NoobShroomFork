When selecting skills, prioritize those that enhance your survivability or boost your pals' damage output.
Be careful, many skills, such as Worldly Snare, only improve the Beastmaster’s own stats and do not benefit your pals. Focus on skills that provide direct advantages to your pals or contribute to your overall survivability to maximize your effectiveness in battle.
