Back talents depend on your Divine Feather Coins, so prioritize unlocking nodes in the Tame Beasts tree. Once you've secured the essential nodes there, focus on investing in the HP, DEF, and ATK nodes within the Fury, Archer, and Sorcery trees. Each of these nodes requires 51 Divine Feather Coins to reach level 10, offering substantial boosts: 4400% base ATK, 12000% base DEF, or 6000% base HP. This investment provides greater overall benefits when compared to upgrading the Pal DMG node from level 10 to level 11, which only offers a 10% increase in Pal DMG for 90 Divine Feather Coins.

### PvP Back Talents:

For PvP back talents, the optimal choice within the Tame Beasts tree is the Assisted Combo node. This talent increases your pal's combo rate by 30%, giving each basic attack a 30% chance to strike twice. This provides a significantly higher DPS boost for your pals compared to Crimson Spirit, which increases pal damage by 3% every 2 seconds, up to a maximum of 30%. Since it takes 20 seconds to reach the full 30% increase, the boost is often achieved too late in PvP fights, by which time the battle is usually over.

Additionally, prioritize maxing out Pal Healing. At max level, every 8 pal attacks restore 0.4% of your maximum HP. With the extra combos from Assisted Combo, this healing occurs more frequently, greatly improving your survivability in battle. To further enhance healing, invest in Regeneration to increase your healing rate by 0.01% per level.

Pal Attack Speed should also be maxed out, as it boosts your pal’s attack speed by 20%. This means your pals will hit 9.6 times in the duration it previously took to make 8 attacks, allowing for more frequent healing and increased damage output.

After these priorities, focus on maxing out the Pal Crit DMG node. Since your crit damage percentage is lower, increasing crit damage will result in a larger boost to your overall pal damage. While also increasing Pal DMG is beneficial, ensure you have already upgraded the HP, ATK, and DEF nodes in the Fury, Archery, and Sorcery trees to level 10, as these buffs provide greater overall benefits.
