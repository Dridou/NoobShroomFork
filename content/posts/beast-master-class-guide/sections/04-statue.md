The optimal statue set for maximizing your pal's damage is 5x Global Atk, as they provide a greater overall boost compared to 5x Pal DMG statues. To understand why, let's break down the impact of each type of statue.

Global Atk statue explanation: Your base Global Attack percentage should be around 100%. Equipping 5x SSS Attack statues increases this by 80%, bringing your total Global Attack to 180%. This results in an 80% increase in Global Attack, or 1.8 times the original value.

Pal Dmg statue explanation:In contrast, your base Pal DMG percentage should be around 1300%. With 5x SSS Pal DMG statues, your Pal DMG increases by approximately 250%, resulting in a total of 1550%. This only represents a 19% increase, or 1.19 times the original value.

Thus, the Attack statue has a significantly higher boost when compared to the pal damage statue.
