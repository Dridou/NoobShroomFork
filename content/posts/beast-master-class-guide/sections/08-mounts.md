The ideal mount for the Beastmaster is Hot Wheels ![](/images/mounts/hot-wheels.png), which enhances your pal’s attack speed by 2% per second, up to a maximum of 40%. This mount is highly beneficial as it accelerates your attack rate, leading to more frequent critical hits and combos.

Other excellent mounts to consider include those that boost attack, health, and damage resistance, or provide shielding to mitigate damage. Adapto Slime ![](/images/mounts/adapto-slime.png) is particularly valuable for its shielding capabilities, attack boost, and reduced damage taken. Additionally, the Blue Ox mount is a great choice because its passive effect increases pal crit DMG by 40%.
