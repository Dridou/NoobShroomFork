There are a total of eight main souls, but focusing on four key ones will ensure you achieve both significant damage output and adequate survivability. These crucial souls are Immortal Spirit, Bloodstained Blade, Guardian Star, and Excellent Companion.

- **Immortal Spirit:** Boosts your total health and enhances your HP regeneration rate, allowing you to recover health more quickly each second.
- **Bloodstained Blade:** Enhances your healing rate, which improves the effectiveness of healing triggered by basic attacks, and boosts your global attack, thereby increasing your pal’s basic attack damage.
- **Guardian Star:** Enhances your critical resistance, reduces damage from critical hits, increases your pal's critical rate for more frequent critical hits, and amplifies the damage of those critical hits.
- **Excellent Companion:** While it boosts your counter damage —an effect not beneficial to the Beastmaster— it significantly increases your pal’s damage output.

For additional survivability and damage, consider leveling Forgiving Horns to improve defense in PvP and reduce Boss DMG Res in PvE if you’re struggling with survival. Blade Duo can also be leveled for increased boss damage in PvE.

It is recommended to keep Eye of the Abyss and Wildfire Piercer at level 1, as their effects only benefit the Beastmaster directly and do not influence your pal’s damage output.
