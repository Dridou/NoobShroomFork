It is most effective to prioritize leveling your Pal Seal to enhance pal damage. Additionally, invest in the Global ATK Seal for further damage output and the Global HP Seal to boost survivability. If you require additional survivability, consider leveling the Defense and Crit Resistance Seals slightly. Avoid upgrading the Crit DMG Seal, as it only benefits the Beastmaster, not the pals.![Item two good stats: crit and combo](/images/beastmaster/awakening.png)Typical Awakening repartition
