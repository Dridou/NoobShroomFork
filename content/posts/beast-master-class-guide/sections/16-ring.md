Make sure to choose all Gems of Love from the Cloud Ascension Trials rewards, as upgrading your ring significantly benefits your pals.
At level 50, your pals receive enhanced basic attack crit damage and improved evasion ignore. These bonuses increase with each ring upgrade every 50 levels.

For your information, those are the acquired bonuses for each 50 levels:

- From **lv51**: Increase Pals' Basic Atk Crit Dmg by 15%, Pals' Ignore Evasion by 12%
- From **lv101**: Increase Pals' Basic Atk Crit Dmg by 20%, Pals' Ignore Evasion by 16%
- From **lv151**: Increase Pals' Basic Atk Crit Dmg by 30%, Pals' Ignore Evasion by 20%
