### Resume

The Beastmaster emphasizes seven key stats to optimize their effectiveness: Pal Crit Chance, Pal Crit DMG, Pal DMG, Global ATK, Pal Combo Chance, Pal ATK Speed, Health, and Regeneration. Stats that enhance pal crit rate, pal combo chance, pal attack speed, pal crit damage, and pal evasion ignore are displayed directly on the pals themselves rather than in the general stat window.

### Damage statistics

Pal Damage represents the amount of damage your pals inflict with their basic attacks. Pal Crit Chance determines the likelihood of these attacks landing critical hits, while Pal Crit DMG enhances the damage of those critical strikes.

However, Pal Crit DMG isn’t the only factor that boosts your pals' basic attacks. The Beastmaster’s Final Attack also contributes to increasing this damage. For a comprehensive understanding, refer to the Pal Basic Attack DMG formula below.

Pal Combo Chance may trigger when a pal's basic attack hits a target. If triggered, a second attack will strike the same target. We are currently determining whether effects or skills activated by the initial basic attack also apply to this additional combo attack. We will provide updates once we have more information. The damage for this combo attack is calculated using the Pal Combo DMG Formula.

It’s important to note that the Beastmaster’s own Crit DMG and Combo DMG do not influence the damage of pal basic attacks. Only Pal Crit DMG and Pal Combo DMG impact this damage. The sole statistic that reduces pal basic attack damage is Pal Resistance.

#### Pal Basic Attack DMG Formula

Pal Basic Attack = (Character Final Attack - Target Defense) x Pal DMG Multiplier x Pal’s DMG Attribute

#### Pal Combo DMG Formula

Pal Basic Attack = (Character Final Attack - Target Defense) x Pal DMG Multiplier x Pal’s DMG Attribute x Pal Combo DMG

### Defensive Stats

The Beastmaster relies on two primary stats to maintain their survivability: Health, which boosts their total hit points, and Regeneration, which enhances the rate at which they recover health per second. Additionally, for PvP scenarios, focusing on Defense can be beneficial. Although it's a secondary consideration, increasing Defense can reduce the amount of damage taken, offering an extra layer of protection. If you find yourself struggling to stay alive despite high Health and Regeneration, investing in Defense can provide the necessary resilience to improve your survival.
