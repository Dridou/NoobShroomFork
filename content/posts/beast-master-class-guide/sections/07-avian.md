For optimal performance, equip the Avian Yoghurt Champ ![Yoghurt champ avian](/images/beastmaster/avian-yoghurt-champ.png) for its active skill, which boosts your pal's damage output significantly. This skill increases the final crit DMG by 2% for each pal crit, stacking up to 5 times, and similarly boosts final combo DMG by 2% for each pal combo, also stacking up to 5 times. After achieving 5 pal crits, your pal crit DMG will increase by 10%, and after 5 pal combos, your pal combo DMG will also rise by 10%.

To maximize the benefits of this Avian, you should enhance it with the following four passive affixes: Divine Touch, Burst Impact, Super League, and either Loss of Control or All-Around Control. Divine Touch provides a substantial DPS boost by giving your pal crits a 30% chance to inflict additional damage equal to 0.5% of the target's current HP. Meanwhile, Burst Impact, Super League, and Loss of Control (or All-Around Control) further amplify your pal’s crit damage, enhancing their overall effectiveness.
