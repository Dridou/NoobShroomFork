### Beast Master characteristics

The Beastmaster is one of the two subclasses available for the Spirit Channeler class, specializing in enhancing pal damage. This subclass excels at increasing your pal’s critical rate, boosting their damage output, and enabling them to bypass evasion.
Additionally, the Beastmaster's own health loss translates into a significant increase in their pal's damage per second (DPS), amplifying their offensive capabilities even further.
