The best artifact for the Beastmaster is Eternal Flame, as it summons a companion to absorb damage on your behalf and boosts your pal's final crit DMG. This is the only artifact that directly increases pal damage.

However, other artifacts can also be beneficial. For instance, Siren’s Whisper provides a summon that not only absorbs damage but also deals damage, offering additional utility.

If you’re looking for a free-to-play option, Chaotic Warlord’s Hammer is currently available. While it provides only a modest boost to your damage, it is worth acquiring with your mushroom tokens for its 10% Global Basic Attack DMG bonus, especially if you already have a suitable mount.
