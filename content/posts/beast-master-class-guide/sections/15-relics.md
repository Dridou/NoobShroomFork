The relics you choose should align with the specific pals and skills you use, so they may vary from the examples provided in this guide. When leveling your relics, it's generally efficient to upgrade them all to the same level initially, as this approach costs fewer relic shards at lower levels and ensures a consistent increase in passive effects for base ATK, DEF, and HP. However, some relics, such as the Magic Box, enhance the percentage effectiveness of skills associated with the artifact. In these cases, it may be advantageous to level these relics higher than others to maximize their benefits.

### PvE Relics

Currently, I haven't found a set of PvE relics that outperforms the PvP relics for dealing damage to bosses or waves, except for adjustments to the book slot. For boss encounters, you should consider using the Beasthide Book for increased boss damage, the Immunity Book for enhanced survivability, or the Healing Book, which allows you to lose and regain HP, thereby boosting damage through the Beastmaster class passive.

For wave content, the choice between the Healing Book, Immunity Book, or Flame Book will depend on your specific needs. The Healing Book aids in maintaining HP and maximizing damage output, while the Immunity and Flame Books provide different benefits based on your current strategy.
