### Rune sets

The optimal set bonuses are 4xGlobal Pal DMG Bonus for a 15% increase in pal DMG, and 2*Crit Res for a 5% boost in Crit Res.

### Main affixes

So, for the changing top right and bottom right rune, you want respectively Pal Dmg top right and Crit res bottom right

### Sub-affixes

Concerning sub-affixes:

- Pal Crit Dmg
- Pal Dmg
- Crit Rate
- Basic Atk res

These combinations will enhance both your damage output and survivability, ensuring your Beastmaster performs at its best.
