Focus primarily on acquiring hammers. If you manage to reach 100 points, you should have already obtained the event's back accessory. If not, seek advice on which back accessory to prioritize based on its specific benefits.
Each event grants points in different units, but the shop remains consistent, offering primarily hammers for 10 points, the event's back accessory for 100 points, and an artifact for 200 points. As a free-to-play (F2P) player, you will typically earn around 10-20 points per event. With a full battle pass, you can accumulate approximately 60 points. In these cases, it’s best to prioritize purchasing hammers without hesitation, as they provide significant value.
If you purchase certain packs and surpass the 100-point threshold, you should ideally have obtained the artifact unless you’re particularly unlucky. If you have already secured the artifact, continue focusing on buying hammers—they are extremely cost-effective, providing up to 10,000 hammers and substantial stat bonuses.
However, if you have not obtained the artifact, the decision becomes more nuanced and highly dependent on the specific artifact in question. This scenario requires a more personalized approach, so don’t hesitate to seek advice on Discord or conduct further research to determine the best course of action.
