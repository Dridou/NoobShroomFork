The Brawl Shop is directly linked to Guild Wars and offers a unique opportunity for players. Here, you can purchase a Red Soul 1 of your choice, making it the only place in the game where you can collect all 8 Red Souls. Remember, once you have a Red Soul 1 of each type, subsequent Red Souls can be used to try for better substats on your Pink Souls. Additionally, the Brawl Shop is the only place where you can buy certain spells that are not obtainable through skill tickets.
