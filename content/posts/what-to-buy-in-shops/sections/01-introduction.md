Keep in mind that different players will naturally have different preferences. Depending on your objectives, some choices may be better than others. These are just general suggestions for items that are usually good options if you're unsure what to choose. While they might not always be the absolute best, they are solid choices.

### How to read these tables:

- mandatory: This item is essential in the game and should be your primary goal in the relevant shop.
- always: You should always take this item whenever you can without hesitation. This tag may be accompanied by "from game_progression", indicating you should only take it starting from a certain point.
- important: This is an important item for your game progression, and I recommend getting it.
- rarely: You often won’t have enough points to purchase this, so it’s better considered only if you have extra points.
- situational: These items can be very good, but you have to make choices depending on your situation.
- never: I advise you never to take this item.
