The Parking Shop allows you to purchase a variety of bonuses daily, including weekly bonuses for mineral and vegetable harvesting. It also offers unique parking decorations that increase the rewards, money, and remodel guides earned by all mounts parked in your lot. Additionally, these decorations provide permanent stat boosts when you acquire and upgrade them, making the Parking Shop a valuable resource for enhancing your in-game experience.
