Just a reminder: You earn your guild points through various activities, including:

- Completing the Lava Boss event, which can be done every 8 hours.
- Participating in the Grumpy event.
- Getting additional bonus points when you are online during one of the two daily sessions.
- Earning points when your guild defeats a level of the Grumpy event.
- Helping your guildmates, which can earn you up to 500 points per day.
- Making donations, which gives you 500 total points.
- And probably a few other ways I might have missed.

These points can be spent in the guild shop. Here’s a list of what to buy in order of priority. Keep in mind that some items may not be unlocked depending on your guild level. As you progress in the game, you will earn more guild points daily (around 10,000 to 15,000 points).
