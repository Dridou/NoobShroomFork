The Cross Server Shop is the only place where you can obtain Awakening Crystals and Red soul 2 (as f2p player) and you must obtain your 6 crystals in priority. It is recommended to buy the 500 lamps every day, even if it slows down your progression a bit. In the long run, you will be awakened and catch up with your peers by having a much larger number of lamps opened. Remember, in PvP, your account level relative to your opponent’s level affects both the damage you receive and the damage you deal.

After obtaining your 6 crystals, you must aim to obtain at least your essential Rose Souls as quickly as possible (refer to each class guide to know which ones to prioritize). It is also recommended to continue collecting lamps.
