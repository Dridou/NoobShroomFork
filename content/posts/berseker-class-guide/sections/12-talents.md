For Berserker you absolutely want the counter damage node in the Fury branch. We also want to get 5 to 10 points in the Combo Dmg Res node.

Then you have multiple strategies depending on what you want:

- PvE Res build (especially for grumpy): get the Basic Atk Resistance nodes to the maximum, only putting points in the required nodes in the Archery blue path, and in the Sorcery Yellow path, for waves, Cloud Ascension Trial, Chrono Tower and so on. Most of monsters only hit with basic attacks, including most bosses. With the update it's harder to get basic attack resistance so you can try an evasion build for PvE.
- PvP ballanced build: get the Skill Dmg Resistance and/or Counter Dmg Resistance nodes depending on the fights where you struggle. Associated with your artefact right rune you can achieve ~35% counter resistance or ~35% combo resistance for example. Focus hard on CritDamage and Counter Damage since you aim to hit hard your opponent.

You have plenty other strategies, but these are some useful ones, join our discord to discuss about them and disover new ones !
Also, don't forget to have fun, you can infinitely reset these talents for free so why not try different strategies ?
