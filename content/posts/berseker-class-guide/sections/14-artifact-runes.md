For reminder, you should first consider reading Artifact Rune System

Sets: at first don't bother with sets and focus on substats,
then you must try to get 4 Crit Dmg runes , since this is the only place we can get global crit and it'll give you +15% global Crit Damage. And 2 counter runes to get +5% global counter damage.

You must absolutely:

- get a top right Counter Dmg rune
- get a bottom right Crit Dmg rune

Know that evasion allows you to counter attack too, For the substats, you want to focus on these stats:

- (S tier) Counter Dmg
- (S tier) Res Skill Damage>
- (S- tier) Res Combo Damage
- (A+ tier) Res Counter Damage
- (A tier) Ignore Evasion
- (B tier) Evasion
