From immortal equipment, you want to start focusing on substats.
Each time you'll start to get good stats on your equipment, once comes the moment you have to up its rarity.
Especially for gold equipements, you want to have at least Counter or CC before swapping, but ALWAYS take Counter + CC, even one lower gear rank.![Item from Warbringer with two desired stats](/images/warbringer-item-compare-good.png)The two stats we want![Item from Warbringer with only one good stat](/images/warbringer-item-compare-bad.png)Only one good stat
