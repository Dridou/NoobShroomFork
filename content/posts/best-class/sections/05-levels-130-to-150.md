By this point, Eternal Gear starts becoming available, offering a significant boost to your HP and overall survivability. This is where regeneration builds come into play, allowing classes like Prophet to dominate with cooldown reduction and strong healing.

Continue focusing on PvE with your Crossbowman, as it remains one of the best for clearing high-level content.

For PvP, Prophet regeneration builds and cooldown reduction become increasingly powerful. Berserker's dominance fades slightly during this phase, while Crossbow builds become a bit more challenging due to the competition.
