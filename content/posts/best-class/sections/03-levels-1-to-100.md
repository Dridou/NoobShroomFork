For early game progression, the Mage class, specifically the Prophet, stands out as the fastest and easiest option for PvE. Its healing capabilities make it a reliable pick for surviving early game content while pushing through levels efficiently. As you get closer to level 100, switching to a Necromancer build can also work well for PvE.

In contrast, Berserker shines in PvP at these levels with its aggressive playstyle, but it’s not wise to focus on PvP early on. Instead, prioritize PvE content to gather resources quickly and power up.
