In summary, it’s crucial to have a Crossbowman for PvE progression, as they remain a reliable option throughout the game. For PvP, the focus tends to shift towards Prophets, but they may require some premium artifacts to reach their full potential. That said, a solid regeneration build can still be effective without heavy investment.

And don’t forget—you can change your class at any time for just 4,000 red diamonds, which isn’t too expensive. Also, with the ability to reset your awakening nodes for free, the game gives you plenty of flexibility to try out different builds.

Lastly, make sure to gear up properly for each class when you drop a new gear. With the introduction of 10 equipment sets, you can now prepare two full sets for every class to ensure you're always ready for any situation!
