At the highest levels, PvP shifts into a battle of optimization and gear quality. The most powerful builds revolve around:

- **Crossbowman**: When exceptionally geared, they hit hard and have the talents necessary to be formidable in both PvE and PvP.
- **Prophets**: With high cooldown reduction and massive HP pools, they become a difficult matchup to counter in PvP.
- **Berserkers**: Strong critical damage and counter mechanics make them viable in PvP, but they require significant investment in gear to be competitive.
- **Beast Masters**: These start to rise in popularity, especially with well-leveled acolytes and the right talents to support their playstyle.
