Each day you have 5 tickets, allowing for 5 possible attacks up to a maximum of 10 attacks. It is important to understand that even if you win all 5 attacks, at higher levels, players buy extra tickets. If you win all your matches, you might not be in the top 10 as those players will have too many points to catch up with. This is why I recommend buying 3 tickets (as a guideline) per day.
