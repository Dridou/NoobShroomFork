You can buy up to 3 super miner cards ![](/images/super-mining-image.png), each granting 50% more resources for 50 uses within 1 hour (the timer stops if you leave the game, while the pickaxes come back).
A crucial tip: if you use a bomb or a drill on multiple cells, it only counts as one use of the card!
