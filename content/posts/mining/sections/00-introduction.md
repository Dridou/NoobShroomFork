We often feel too lazy to use our pickaxes and mine manually, but this is one of the key elements of power that allows top players to widen the gap. Currently, there is no limit (999) to the level of the final nodes that provide stats. That's why it's crucial to optimize your mining as much as possible and avoid relying on auto-mining (though we all have done it once or twice, we know how tempting it can be...).
