You can buy drills ![Drill icon](../images/drill.png) and bombs ![Bomb icon](../images/bomb.png) in the family shop, which are very important for efficient mining. Additionally, you can get 2 drills and 2 bombs for free by watching ads as showed below:

![Drill and bomb free with ads](../images/mine-bonuses.png)
