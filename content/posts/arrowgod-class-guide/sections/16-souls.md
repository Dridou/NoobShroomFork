### Best Pink soul acquisition order

The 'default' way and the most balanced one is:

ATK &gt; COMBO &gt; HP &gt; DEF &gt; BASIC ATK &gt; others

Note that you can take Combo first if you prioritize PvE content to get more rewards, though in early game, ATK pink soul allow to transform the effect into global attack % which is as essential as getting the Boss Dmg % stat from Combo Pink soul.

*"Why we don't take Basic Atk soul before others since Plume deals a lot of basic attacks ?"*

*-&gt;****Pink souls do not upgrade the stats already given in the red form****. Instead, they add another statistic to the soul, for example Combo Soul add 'Boss Dmg' with the pink version of it. And Basic Atk Pink Soul adds 'Skill Dmg' stat, which is not a priority as an Archer.*

### Souls levels ratio

For the soul levels, and assuming you have every rune in red rarity (else it prioritize the red offensive souls you have), here is a good balanced example (updated):

- **lv45** | ATK
- **lv40** | Combo Dmg
- **lv40** | Crit Dmg
- **lv40** | HP
- **lv35** | Res Crit Damage
- **lv30** | DEF
- **lv1** | Basic Atk
- **lv1**| Counter Dmg

*++Side note:++*

In early game, level up your Atk and Combo soul in priority.
In mid game, you can take the same ratios dividing every level by /2 (~23 Atk, 20 Combo, 15 def...)

*++Souls Tips:++*

Since it is possible to reset souls, It is advised to take 1/2 days per week where **you reset all your souls and only level up your Atk/CritDmg/Combo souls** (Thursday -&gt; no cross server PvP) and try to push the PvE content as much as possible, then reverting to a balanced set.
