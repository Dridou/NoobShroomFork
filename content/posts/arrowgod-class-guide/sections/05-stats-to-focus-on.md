To optimize your build, prioritize **Crit** and **Combo** stats, idealy more **Combo** then **Crit**. A valuable sub-stat to look for is **Regeneration**.

With artifacts like the **Panda Bow** providing +20% Crit Rate, you can afford to sacrifice a bit of Crit Rate from your gear to achieve around **1% to 1.5%**Regeneration. This setup allows you to maintain strong, consistent damage output while giving you enough sustain to tank the Prophet&rsquo;s burst or **endure the Cheetah mount's burst**, which can be crucial for defeating your opponent&mdash;especially if you don&rsquo;t have the Cheetah mount yourself.
