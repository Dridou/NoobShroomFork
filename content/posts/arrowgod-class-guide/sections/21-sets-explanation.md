### Rules for every set

**For all the sets presented**, these elements remain the same:

- Statue: 5x combo
- Gear: Always the same gear (with a maximum of crit rate and combo rate)
- Relics: Except the book one, you'll choose combo or crit mask according to your needs, Thundercaller Kite, Magic box, Time statue (95% of the time), and Storm necklace (95% of the time).

### Pals alternatives

#### If you don't own a certain pal:

If you miss some pals, take the same family pal of a lower rarity.

Example: if you don't have the red Hero Bird ![Hero Bird pal icon](../images/aco-hero-bird.png), take either the ![Owl pal icon](../images/aco-chouette.png) or the ![Blue bird icon](../images/aco-blue-bird.png)

#### SEA players:

ASIA players: replace ![Hero Bird pal icon](../images/aco-hero-bird.png) with ![Fiery tail pal icon](../images/aco-fiery-tail.png)

#### ![Toothpick pal icon](../images/aco-aubergine.png) Toothpick alternative:

**EU players:** take the ![Benny pal icon](../images/aco-benny.png) but be careful: initially when the boss appears, it focuses on your character and walks toward it until being in range to hit him. When you bump/launch/airborne the boss, it changes the boss's focus to your clone, and so the boss doesn't walk as far as it should to hit your character and may kill your clone before the disarm triggers.

**SEA players:** If I'm not wrong, you have the toothpick for free, so simply use it.

#### ![Red Banana pal icon](../images/aco-banana.png) Red Banana alternative:

Simply replace it with the blue banana ![Blue Banana pal icon](../images/aco-blue-banana.png), half the combo rate but still very useful.
