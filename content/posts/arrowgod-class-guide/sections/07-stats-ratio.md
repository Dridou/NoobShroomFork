### Early / mid game (until 50m power):

Concerning Plume Monarch, we really want to have a good balance between our Combo % and our Crit Rate % or CC for short.

Usually, you want to aim at 80% Combo and at least 50% Crit Rate, but this can change depending of your equipment and your level. The more you have CC damage the more you should balance your Combo and CC equally (with always combo in priority).

Here's a list of good ratios:

- 80% Combo / 50% CC
- 85% Combo / 55% CC
- 90% Combo / 65% CC
- 100% Combo / max possible CC

As crossbow, the mask relic allows up to exchange 10% Combo for 10% CC and vice versa, use this to be more versatile and build a good ratio

Some examples of ratios comparison, assuming you have a decent combo and crit dmg

- 70% Combo / 30% CC &gt; 80% Combo / 20% CC
- 80% Combo / 15% CC &gt; 90% Combo / 5% CC
- 80% Combo / 70% CC &gt; 90% Combo / 60%% CC
- 90% Combo / 50% CC &gt; 100% Combo / 40% CC

### Late game (50/60m+ power):

Once you have decent Gold or a few Eternal pieces, your goal should be to assemble a complete BIS (Best in Slot) set focused on Crit Rate % and Combo %. This means aiming for full Eternal gear with **both Crit Rate % and Combo %** stats.

Additionally, it&rsquo;s beneficial to have a few gear pieces with **Regeneration % and Combo %** (or Regeneration % and Crit Rate %, though that&rsquo;s less optimal). This will allow you to create (or swap gears) a second, hybrid gear setup with Crit Rate mixed with Regeneration, offering a more sustainable build with added survivability.

Disclaimer: a viable sustain build is more adapted to the Hunter class and goes with some specificity, though it's good to keep this in mind while dropping new gears to not throw some potential good gears.
