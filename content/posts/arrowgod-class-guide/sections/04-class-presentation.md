<p>Playing Arrowgod will eventually be necessary to advance in PvE content because of its high and consistent damage output, often referred to as DPS (Damage Per Second). This will help you gather more resources, directly increasing your power.</p>
<p>In PvP, Arrowgod becomes viable when you have enough red feathers to upgrade your back accessory talents and when you own your essential pink souls, allowing you to compete with warriors and mages. We'll go into more detail on this later.</p>
