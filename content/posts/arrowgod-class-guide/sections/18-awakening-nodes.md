Awakening nodes are a way to **adjust your stats** so that you have final efficient stats.
In a general situation you want to prioritize your ATK/ CritDamage / HP / CritDmgRes nodes and let others lv10 (or less) because it is really cheap until lv10.

Here is an example of a equalized awakening nodes distribution for early game:![Crossbow Awakening nodes](../images/crossbow/crossbow-awakening-nodes.png)

With the addition of sets for seals, here are some presets I'd make to use interchangeably between your builds. I'm gonna say what level I (Sloothix this time) have for them and what ratio I'd use.

**Dps**- used for lava and levels, generally anything you want to lots of damage I with no defenses

Atk - 75, this gonna be about 2/3 of ur scrolls and usually double the level of your crit dmg. I like attack higher after multiple brute force tests on lava, don't really have and explanation why but if I had to guess it would be because crit multiplies atk dmg so the ratio of how much crit bonus I get to what I have already is 1/2 the ratio of how much global atk I get compared to how much I have (I only have 2 artifacts excluding hammer), whales might want more crit than atk cause there global atk is already high.

Crit - 33, gonna be about half the level of your atk see above for reason

Combo - 10, free extra dmg basically any higher and it's not free and it's costing Crit or regular atk which is way better

everything else is not leveled

**Pvp**- this is only for PvP against archers and warriors, mage has a different setup I'll share after.

Atk - 52, it's highly recommended by me to put every scroll into atk, unless you can get to a certain milestone of crit or hp (any increment of 500% crit or any increment of 10b hp) without making the level higher than your atk. I have close to 1.8 B atk with this set on.

Hp - 45, this gets me to 210 B hp extra 10b hp, this could probably get lowered and put into atk but I like the extra health. Plume PvP is all a time to kill game and that slight amount of hp not only boosts my dmg a lil cause of 100 slashes and gives me an extra .5s.

Crit - 30, gets me to 7500 crit, anymore would be a waste of scrolls unless it gets me to 8000, which is a good threshold to pass to out do the avg crit res (norm like 2400 on some warriors which gives us 3.3x dmg on crit, [900 - 1200](tel:900-1200) on a plume which is about 8x dmg on crit)

All the other ones at ten, all useful stats but not worth investing in. First ten levels are super cheap.
