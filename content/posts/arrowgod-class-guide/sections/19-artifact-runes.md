As a reminder, you should first consider reading the [Artifact Rune System](../tips/artifact-rune-system) to understand how it works and what is important to do when you build / choose which rune to upgrade.

**From early to late game:**

Get **a top right Combo Runes and bottom right Crit Damage one.**(when this is not done by players, this is the most common issue I see from crossbow asking for help)

**Early game:**

As a **beginner**, simply **don't level your runes beyond level 12** unless you're sure it's a great rune. You'll lose very little experience when upgrading a better rune later on by feeding the previous one into it.

**Mid / Late game:**

After that, aim to get a set of **4 Global Crit runes**, as this is the only place you can obtain global crit and will provide you +15% global crit damage which is significant.

If possible, aim for a **secondary set of 2 Crit Res runes**, though this is less important. Focus on the 4-piece Global Crit Damage set first.

Your right rune will determine if you want 30% (pink max runed) resistance versus Combos or Counters (not skill res there), it all depends if you want to maximize your chances against archers or warriors.

**Sub-stats:**

At higher levels, you'll naturally deal significant damage, but if you're stunned or airborne (launched), you won't deal any DPS. Also, mages can deal heavy burst damage, and if you die during their burst, it won't matter how much damage you deal.

At higher levels, around 70M, mages will have time to complete a second damage rotation, or Darklords will hit hard with spells that bypass your immunity. That's why surviving is just as important as dealing damage, especially against archers who will bump you.

Let's rank the sub stats as follows:

- **S tier** - Ignore Stun, Ignore Launch -> to avoid being controlled
- **A+ tier** - Combo dmg -> we want to hit harder than our opponent and be able to deal with reges mages
- **A tier** - Skill Dmg Res, Basic Atk Dmg res -> resistances hard to get elsewhere
- **A tier** - Launch (airborne) -> can make the difference in a short aggressive fight versus archers
- **B tier** - Counter Dmg Res, Combo Dmg Res -> Scales very good as your HP grow
- **B tier** - Evasion, Ignore evasion

With Eternal Gears, fights will last much longer, and while we want to deal big damage, it's equally important to survive in PvP.
