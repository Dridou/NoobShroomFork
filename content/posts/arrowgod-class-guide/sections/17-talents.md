At the end of this guide you'll find [recommended sets](arrowgod-class-guide#sets-explanation) along with corresponding talent trees for each.

These talent sets are shown in the [Talent Generator](talent-generator) tailored to specific Feather counts and optimized for various strategies or dungeons.

++As a++**++Core Build ( &lt;6000 feathers):++**

You want to aim for **Combo and Crit Damage Nodes**which are the main part of Plume Class Damages. And upgrading all your base Stats nodes to lv10.

Here is the Core Build for any Archer / Arrowgod having less than 6000 feathers: [Core Build Talents](talent-generator?class=Plume%20Monarch&build=cm2ugpl420001v0tau66bovz7)

*++"Why until 6000 feathers?"++*

*-&gt; Activating a final node requires 5,763 feathers to unlock the entire corresponding branch with minimum points in each node. However, it&rsquo;s generally****not worth investing all your feathers into a single branch****without first allocating at least 10/20 points in other base stat nodes.*

*A more optimal strategy is to****max out your Combo and Crit Damage nodes****while ensuring 10 to 20 points in base stat nodes, as this approach maximizes your damage output.*

*++"How do I distribute my feathers if I have much less than 6000 ?"++*

*-&gt; For a minimal talent setup, start by activating the Archer&rsquo;s Yellow branch with 10/20 points in base stat nodes and 10/20 points in Combo and Crit Damage nodes. This will boost your damage significantly with a minimal feather investment.*

*Next, allocate 10 to 20 points in the base stat nodes of the other trees, and finally, max out your Combo and Crit Damage nodes to 20/20. This progression will lead you to the Core Talent Build.*

"*++What if I have way MORE than 6000?"++*

-&gt; *Than that means your ready to add more than one final node, for every 6000 feathers you have you can get one final node. Now just because you have enough doesn't mean it's good to instantly switch over. There's some boxes I'd check off first, depending on the set your thinking about (PvP, dps, etc..) make sure you can get all the important regular nodes to a decent level so you won't drop all your stats for one final node. For PvP make sure you have your crit and combo dmg up and some res, and dps make sure you can get max skill regen spd and max dmg nodes (crit/combo/atkspd)*

*As for the order of final nodes id take*

*Gale barage &gt; Eager Momentum &gt; Temporal compression &gt; then depending on the build you can choose between ascension for more dmg for long fights, Stun +15% for theatre, or any other one you think might benefit more. There is no perfect 4th final node.*
