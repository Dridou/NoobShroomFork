Redeeming codes is quick and takes less than a minute.

![Open your profile](../images/double-profile-click.png)
![Redeem code button](../images/redeem-code-button.png)

### Steps

1. Tap your profile picture.
1. Tap your profile picture again to open the profile screen.
1. Tap the **Redeem Code** button.
1. Enter the code and tap **Exchange**.

### Troubleshooting

- Codes are case-sensitive. Try exact capitalization.
- If a code fails, it may be expired or already claimed.
- Some codes are limited by server, region, or event time.
