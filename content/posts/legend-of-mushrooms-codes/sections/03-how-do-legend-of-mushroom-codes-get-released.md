Legend of Mushroom codes are usually shared by the game's team when they want to mark an update, promote a campaign, or encourage players to join community channels. Most codes are posted publicly, but some are distributed in smaller batches through partners or limited-time activities.

Because codes are often tied to a specific promotion, they can expire quickly. Some may only work on certain regions or servers, and many are case sensitive, so entering the exact letters, numbers, and symbols matters.

Rewards can also be unclear at first. A code might be announced before the in-game mail is delivered, the reward list may be summarized differently on each platform, or the contents may vary if the code is designed for a specific group (such as new players, a region, or an event). That's why two sources can describe the "same" code differently, or why your results may not match someone else's.

Check back often, since new codes can appear at any time and older ones may stop working without notice.

- Official social posts (Facebook, X, Instagram, etc.)
- Official Discord announcements and pinned messages
- Collaborations with other games, brands, or platforms
- In-game or seasonal events
- Creator/private drops (streamers, partners, community giveaways)
