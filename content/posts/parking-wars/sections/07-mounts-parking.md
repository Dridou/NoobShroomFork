You can park a total of six mounts: five in parkings within your server, with a maximum of one in public parking and four in private parkings. Additionally, you can park one mount in the inter-server public parking.

These mounts can be parked for a maximum of 12 hours per day, but only for 8 consecutive hours at a time.
