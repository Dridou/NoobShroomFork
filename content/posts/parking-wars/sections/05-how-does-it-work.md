In your own parking, you can set various bonuses including drop bonuses, coin bonuses, and remodal coin bonuses.

You can assign a fee ranging from 5% to 20% that other players will pay (or choose not to pay) when they park in your parking. If they choose to pay, they can opt to be defended by you, the owner, and will not be pillaged by you. This system is reciprocal when you park in someone else's parking.
