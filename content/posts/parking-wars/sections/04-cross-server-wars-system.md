Inter-server parking wars involve competing with three other servers. The server that generates the most parking coins by the end of the week (Sunday at midnight) wins the war. Parking coins are generated when a player from your server parks a mount in a parking lot owned by your server. Therefore, it's crucial to have as many parkings as possible occupied during the night.
