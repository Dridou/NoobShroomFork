Attacks are permitted between 8 AM and midnight. During the rest of the time, each server retains control of its parkings, and they are not attackable. A parking lot with no defenders, when attacked, will be taken over by the server with the most successful attacks. Once a parking lot is captured, it becomes unattackable for 4 hours.
