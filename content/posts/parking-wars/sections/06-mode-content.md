Parking Wars is a crucial aspect of the game, providing significant benefits and rewards enhancing your decorations and giving you bonus stats.

Moreover, engaging in Parking Wars offers substantial rewards, including exclusive decorations and resources, making it an essential feature to master for both competitive and strategic advantages.
