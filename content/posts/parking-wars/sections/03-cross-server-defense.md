You can defend a parking lot by joining the defense queue.

You can move to the top of the queue at any time, which is key to being attacked by an opponent you can defeat to improve your personal ranking.

When parking a mount in the inter-server public parking, an alert will prompt you to decide if you want to defend the parking; select 'no' because you want to defend a parking that is about to be attacked or is currently under attack to surprise your opponent and ensure victory.

You can withdraw from the defense of one parking to move to another, but there is a 30-minute cooldown, and you retain your debuffs.
