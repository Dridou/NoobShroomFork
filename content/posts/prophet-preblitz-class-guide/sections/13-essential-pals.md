| Pal | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| ![Red Slime pal icon](/images/aco-red-slime.png) | Grants a 25% bonus to skill cooldown reduction (in addition to the Prophet class's 30% bonus). This is essential for any build to ensure you can use your spells at least a second time. |
| ![Fiery Tail pal icon](/images/aco-fiery-tail.png) | Increases skill damage by 60%, useful in mid-game but replaceable later (it's another pal for SEA players). |
| ![Hero Bird pal icon](/images/aco-hero-bird.png) | Increases attack speed, essential for pre-blitz strategy. Faster attack speed reduces casting time, increasing your chances to damage your opponent before they cast their blitz spell. |
| ![Cat Prince pal icon](/images/aco-cat-prince.png) | Procs with the pumpkin and bat, dealing great damage. |
| ![Dragon pal icon](/images/aco-dragon.png) | Increases base crit damage by 50% and skill crit rate by 20%, a must-have for Prophets. |
| ![Snail pal icon](/images/aco-snail.png) | Reduces enemy movement speed, only in PvE when combined with Thorns, giving you more time to deal damage. |
| ![Fox pal icon](/images/aco-fox.png) | Great for PvP with a decent stun rate, can be combined with ![Dazzled skill icon](/images/skill-dazzled.png) |
| ![Deer pal icon](/images/aco-deer.png) | Good for PvP but not for pre-blitz build, also useful in PvE. |
