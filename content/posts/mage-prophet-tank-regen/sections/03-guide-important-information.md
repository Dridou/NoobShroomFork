Disclaimer: In this guide, I will walk you through my approach to playing this type of mage. However, please note that the build only becomes truly viable once you acquire Eternal gear. This is because Eternal gear provides a significant boost to your HP, which greatly enhances your regeneration abilities.

### How does regeneration stat works ?

It's very important to understand how regeneration works.

Your regeneration is a % between 0 and ~7%. It means you'll recover this % of your max HP per second. Though in PvP environment this regeneration is reduced to 30% of its original value, which still can be a 2% per second which is huge.

### What can influence your regeneration stat?

![Regeneration system](/images/mage-regen/regen-system.png)

This regeneration is based on your total regeneration, also called based regen in some places of the game. On top of that is applied your HP regen bonus.

#### Here is what influence your **base regeneration**:

- **Equip** - The sum of your regeneration provided by your gears, maximum 2.5% (0.25 on your 10 gears)
- **Partner** - The new pal Floral Pandan (not relased in SEA server at the moment) ![Floral Panda](/images/aco-floral-panda.png) allows to get 0.1% base regen
- **Back Accessory** - Three nodes are able to provide you regeneration for your information
- ![Regeneration node](/images/mage-regen/regen-node-1.png)Regeneration node
- ![Regeneration node detail](/images/mage-regen/regen-node-2.png)Regeneration node detail
- ![Regeneration node from beast tree](/images/mage-regen/regen-beast-node-1.png)Regeneration node from beast tree
- ![Regeneration node detail from beast tree](/images/mage-regen/regen-beast-node-2.png)Regeneration node detail from beast tree
- ![Regeneration node from fury tree](/images/mage-regen/regen-final-node-1.png)Regeneration node from fury tree
- ![Regeneration node detail from fury tree](/images/mage-regen/regen-final-node-2.png)Regeneration node detail from fury tree

#### And here is what influence your **HP regen**:

- **Partner** - The Kung Fu master pal (for EU Server)![](/images/aco-panda.png) allows to get +40% of HP Regen.
- **Soul** - When your HP soul is Pink and levels up, it upgrades your HP Regeneration.
- **Avian** - The affix 'Regen Ability' gives you some HP Regen bonus, starting at 10%![Regeneration affix](/images/mage-regen/hp-regen-avian.png)
