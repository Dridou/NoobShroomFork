The *Prophet* Mage excels in a unique playstyle known as 'regeneration' This build focuses on absorbing as much damage as possible while gradually dealing consistent damage to defeat enemies. By sustaining themselves with regenerative abilities, they can endure long battles, making them particularly effective in PvP combat.
