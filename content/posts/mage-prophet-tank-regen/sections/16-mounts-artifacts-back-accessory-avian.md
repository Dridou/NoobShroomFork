### For a starting point for **free-to-play players**

**Mounts:** Take the **cloud** first, the essential main mount for mage with its 10% Skill Crit Rate increase.

**Back Accessory:** Emerald Embrace (the only free back accessory available through the wheel).

**Avians S tier for regen strategy:** Dense Cloud

### For **cashers**

**Mounts:** The Diving Duck as it reduce the attack of the ennemy.

**Back Accessory:** Shell Shade is undoubtedly the best option for a Prophet and an excellent choice for other classes as well.

**Artifact:** Siren's whisper or as a second choice Eternal Flame both give you a free time without damage to regen.

**Avians S tier for regen strategy:** Dense Cloud
