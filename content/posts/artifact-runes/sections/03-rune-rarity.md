Runes come in various rarities, each represented by a different color from blue to pink (highter). Higher rarity runes require significantly more experience to level up but also have more initial statistics, their main statistic will be the highest at the maximum level and the improvement of the sub-statistics will be higher every 4 levels, let's see how the leveling of runes works.
