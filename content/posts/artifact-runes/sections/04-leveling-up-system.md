Every rune is obtained lv1 and can be level up at the maximum to lv20 by giving it other runes.

When you give a rune to another, the target rune not only receives the base experience of the sacrificed rune (the amount varying according to its rarity), but it also acquires a portion of the experience accumulated by the latter. Thus, if you transfer a level 16 rune to a level 1 rune, the level 1 rune will receive a portion of the total experience of the level 16 rune.

![New stat apparition example](../images/sacrifice-rune.png)
A new stat (ignore airborne) appeared.

How does it work ?

When you give a rune to another, you definitely loose the given rune and the new one obtain a % of the experience:

- if the given rune is level 10 or below: no experience is lost, it allows you to test out different runes to see how they level up.
- else, you will loose between 5% and 25% of the experience, here as you can see sacrificing a lv20 pink rune on another pink only levels up this one lv17.
