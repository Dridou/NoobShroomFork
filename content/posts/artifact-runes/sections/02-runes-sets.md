We have six available rune slots, and currently, there are six different types of runes. Each set of two identical runes grants a 5% bonus, while a set of four identical runes grants an additional 10% bonus specific to the rune type, making a 15% total bonus.

Here are the different types of sets and their corresponding bonuses:

- Heart of Resilience: Global Counter Damage
- Furious Gale: Global Combo Damage
- Mana Mastery: Global Basic Attack
- Blazing Roar: Global Crit Damage
- Iron Wall: Global Crit Resistance
- Elemental Wrath: Global Skill Damage
