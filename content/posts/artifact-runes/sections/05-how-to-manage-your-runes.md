Runes can only be obtained through the Flames Dungeon. This dungeon ranges from level 1 to level 25. It is crucial to reach level 20 as quickly as possible, as it is the only level where you can obtain pink rarity runes. Then you want to reach lv25 to get more runes per run, which also means more food to level up your runes.

Then to modify your runes you need to click on your artifact place, the 'Enchant' tab and to click on 'Change'.
