Let's say your character has an attack speed of 4, meaning it performs 4 basic attacks per second, more projectiles are sent (Crossbow passive, Combos, Counterstrikes, gatling 'bullets' and so on) but you only hit |your atk speed| * basic attacks per second, those are the only ones that can trigger a combo, a counter, a crit, a stun, or an airborne effect, and only those can heal you.
