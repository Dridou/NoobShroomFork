At its maximum level (150), the dungeon gives you 800 lamps per run, with 5 more lamps added per level.

Lamps are very important because they help you obtain better gear, but you probably already know that. However, few players realize that the stats of equipment drastically increase every 10 levels (lv131, 141, 151, etc.), especially eternal equipment's HP, which increases by 100k HP every 10 levels.

Also, in PvP combat, the level difference between you and your opponent slightly affects the damage you deal to them and vice versa.
