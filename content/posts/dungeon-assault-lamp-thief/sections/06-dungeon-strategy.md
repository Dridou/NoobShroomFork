In general, you should equip your most offensive gear for this dungeon. Once a week, try to create a personalized set focused on damage to push your limits in the dungeon. You can also modify your souls to completely abandon defensive stats (crit res, defense) and focus on boosting your offensive stats.

Since the boss regenerates 2% HP per second, I tried using an Avian to stop the boss’s regeneration, but it wasn't very successful. However, you can always give it a try.

I would tend to prioritize the crossbow for this dungeon, but warriors deal massive damage. Some players reported progressing further as a warrior than as an archer. I suspect the boss has lower resistance to counters than combos, allowing warriors to advance in PvE.
