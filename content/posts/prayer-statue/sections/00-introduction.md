The farm is an essential aspect of the game as it is your only means of obtaining fruit offerings, which grant the best possible stats for your prayer statue.

The concept is simple: you can plant seeds that will take some time to grow, depending on their rarity, before you can harvest them. A normal or rare seed has a certain percentage chance of yielding a normal, rare, or very rare fruit. A very rare seed is guaranteed to produce a very rare fruit. You also have normal and rare fertilizers: ![normal fertilizer](/images/fertilizer-common.png) normal fertilizers accelerate growth by 30 minutes, and rare fertilizers ![rare fertilizer](/images/fertilizer.png) by 1 hour.
