Tips for Farm Optimization:

- Always collect normal fertilizers ![normal fertilizer](/images/fertilizer-common.png) through in-game ads, as you will get one more each time up to a maximum of 11.
- Collect normal seeds in the same way.
- Acquire 3 or 4 rare fertilizers ![rare fertilizer](/images/fertilizer.png) every day with red gems.
