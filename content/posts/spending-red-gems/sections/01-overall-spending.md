### Upgrade your mount

![Clock icon](/images/clock-winter.png) Until your mount reach the rank 7: It is advised to spend the main part (95%) of your diamonds must be invested for buying clocks. The mount is really hard to upgrade and you need to buy a lot of clocks to upgrade it, clocks are a pretty rare resource in the game. You can buy them in the fourth tab of the shop.

### Upgrade your soul

![Soul icon](/images/soul.png) Only when your mount is rank 7 you can stop spending in clocks and buy souls to level up your own souls, which will provide you more bonuses./p><
