A 'Batch Hatch' feature for Flying Pets has been added, along with a "Dismantle" option on the Flying Pet details page.

This update makes managing your Flying Pets much more convenient by allowing multiple pets to hatch at once, which is a great news to gather more Uku fruits.

The dismantling feature also helps in optimizing your collection by allowing you to break down pets you no longer need, don't forget to favorite your best ones in order to create the best possible Avian !
