The Lava Behemoth instance now has a 'Sweep' feature, with rewards based on your highest recorded damage. Defeating the Level 100 Lava Boss unlocks this option.

This new feature makes farming rewards quicker and easier. You could now for example switch to crossbow and / or modify your souls to do a pure DPS build to hit a very high score and then you won't need to worry about that for a few days / week.
