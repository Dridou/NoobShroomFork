- Tapping a character avatar in the Guard Queue now displays player information.
- New debuff icons added to the main and guard interfaces.
- Captain stats in this gameplay, including Combo Multiplier, Counter Multiplier, Basic ATK Multiplier, and Pal DMG, are now scaled to 50% of their original values.
- HP regeneration in this gameplay is now set to 30% of its original value, as in classic PvP Modes

These changes provide better transparency during gameplay. Being able to see opponent stat is a very important feature, especially in Age of Exploration where players can adapt their build depending the opponent.
