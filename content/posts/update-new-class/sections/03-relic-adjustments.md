- **Arrow Bamboo:** Panda Crit +100% → Panda Crit +20% and Crit DMG +300%;
- **Priest Mask:** Evasion +5% → Pal Crit Rate +5%;
- **Whirlwind Feather:** Bird ATK SPD +100% → 60%;
- **Amber Snail:** Snail and Specter DMG +50% → 30%;
- **Blessed Dew:** Cactus Combo Rate +100% → 60%;
- **Magic Box:** Cat and Deer DMG +50% → 30%;
- **Metamorphic Crystal:** Dog, Fox and Octopus ATK SPD +33% → 20%.

### Pre-analysis

The Panda Crit Damage increase to +300% from Arrow Bamboo is a notable change, as it introduces a new way to modify damage coefficients that we haven't seen before with relics. This makes critical hit resistance more crucial for players who may face teams built around maximizing critical hit damage. Players will need to adjust their gear and strategies to counterbalance this potentially powerful new effect.
