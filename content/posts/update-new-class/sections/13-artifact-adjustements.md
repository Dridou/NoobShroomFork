- **Extreme Caution:** Wound all targets within the range, reducing their Regen and Healing Amount by 50% for 2 seconds.

### Pre-analysis

The changes to the **Extreme Caution** artifact are significant. Not only does it now apply to all targets within range rather than just one, but the duration of the effect is doubled from 1 second to 2 seconds. This change makes it particularly effective in 2v2 tournaments and future team-based game modes. The increased duration means that it can now counteract sustained healing from mages and other classes effectively, especially against abilities that provide damage immunity or crowd control immunity like the Blitz skill. It also differentiates the artifact's utility from the **Terminal Strike** and **Multi Shots** avian affixes, which previously had similar effects, thereby giving it a unique role in combat strategy.
