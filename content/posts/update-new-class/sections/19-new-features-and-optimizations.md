### New Features and Optimizations

Several new features and optimizations have been added to enhance your gameplay experience.

#### Sweep Lava Behemoth

You can now sweep the Lava Behemoth Dungeon. Sweep damage is calculated based on your highest damage, and you'll receive rewards corresponding to the damage you deal. Challenge and defeat the Level 100 Lava Boss once to unlock this feature after the update.

#### Batch Summon

To make things easier in the later stages of the game, the Summon Shop now includes a feature that allows you to summon 999 times in one tap with 800 vouchers. This feature unlocks when the corresponding Summon Shop reaches Level 15.

#### Flame Temple

The Flame Temple Dungeon now supports Set Pre-selection. You can choose up to 4 different enchanted sets and you will only get Enchantment Stones for the selected sets after clearing the dungeon. This feature is not active when fewer than 4 sets are selected. Enchantment Stones are sorted to prioritize those that are equipped or locked.

### Pre-analysis

These new features significantly streamline gameplay and reduce the grind associated with endgame content. The ability to sweep dungeons like Lava Behemoth based on your highest damage output not only rewards skillful play but also encourages players to optimize their strategies for maximum damage. The Batch Summon feature in the Summon Shop will be particularly useful for late-game players who need to gather resources efficiently. Meanwhile, the Flame Temple's Set Pre-selection option gives players greater control over their enchantments, ensuring that their efforts are focused on desired upgrades, which is a welcomed quality-of-life improvement.
