### Gameplay Optimizations

We've made several optimizations to improve your overall gameplay experience.

#### Age of Discovery - [Season 2]

The main interface and Guard screen of the Age of Discovery now feature an icon displaying current debuff details.

- In-Combat Captain's Combo Multiplier/Counter Multiplier/Skill DMG/Basic ATK Multiplier/Pal DMG = Out-of-Combat Combo Multiplier * 0.5/Counter Multiplier * 0.5/Skill DMG * 0.5/Basic ATK Multiplier * 0.5/Pal DMG * 0.5
- In-Combat HP Regen Bonus = Out-of-Combat HP Regen Bonus * 0.3
- Tapping on a character's avatar in the Guard queue will now toggle the player information screen

#### Family

Improved the HP display during the battle against the Family Boss Grumpy Bighead and enhanced rewards for Family Treasure Hunt. We have also improved the Family Announcement and Family Brawl Announcement, allowing for edits and modifications to the original text.

#### Parking Wars

New cards added: Dark Trial (increases the number of Dark Trial resets) and Breeding Care (reduces the time required for breeding). The Parking Wars Cross-server Occupation Log now includes information about the player who occupied the spot. The Parking Wars Management menu has a new feature to display a summary of attributes related to the parking mode.

### Pre-analysis

The gameplay optimizations introduced in this update are focused on enhancing the user interface and reducing unnecessary complexity. The changes in Age of Discovery ensure that players have a clear understanding of their debuffs, which is crucial for strategic planning in challenging content. The new Family features and Parking Wars updates add more layers of strategy and provide better rewards, encouraging more participation and engagement in these modes. These improvements reflect the developers' commitment to refining the game and addressing community feedback.
