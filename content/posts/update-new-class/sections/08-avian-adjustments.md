### Pre-analysis

The adjustments to the avian affix **Terminal Strike** align with the changes made to the **Extreme Caution** artifact. This change ensures that the effects are similar, reducing regeneration and healing by 50% in an area of effect. However, there remains a question about whether this change will affect all skills in the area of effect, potentially making it a powerful tool in group battles and team strategies. As players experiment with these changes, it will become clear how impactful these adjustments are on overall gameplay.
