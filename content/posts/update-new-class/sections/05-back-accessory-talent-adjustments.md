Back accessory talents have undergone significant changes, introducing stats that were previously unavailable in this slot, such as Crit DMG bonus, Ignore Launch, Combo Healing, Enemy Energy Regen and Healing Reduction, Energy Regen, and Skill Regen.

- All Back Accessory Talents' Combo DMG, Counter DMG, Basic ATK DMG Multipliers at Max Level: 300% → 500%.
- All Back Accessory Talents' Ignore Combo and Ignore Counter Multipliers at Max Level: 30% → 20%.

### Pre-analysis

The introduction of new stats to back accessory talents significantly broadens the scope of customization and strategy. The increase in multipliers for Combo DMG, Counter DMG, and Basic ATK DMG from 300% to 500% at max level may challenge the current dominance of fixed combo and counter boosts. Additionally, the nerf to Ignore Combo and Ignore Counter talents from 30% to 20% might lead players to explore other talents, potentially diversifying gameplay strategies, especially in PvP settings where these talents are highly valued.
